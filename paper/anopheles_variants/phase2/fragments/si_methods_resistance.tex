\subsection*{Resistance-region panels and matched controls}

The predefined 15-region panel comprised \textit{Vgsc/kdr}, \textit{Rdl}, \textit{Ace1}, \textit{Gste2}, \textit{Cyp6aa/Cyp6p}, \textit{Cyp9k1}, \textit{Cyp4j5}, \textit{Coeae1d}, \textit{Cyp6m2}, \textit{SAP2}, \textit{Cyp4g16}, \textit{Cyp4g17}, \textit{Gstd1}, \textit{Maf-S}, and the adjacent \textit{D7r2/D7r4} region \citep{lucas2023resistance,hancock2024surveillance}. The panel was defined independently of the Phase 2 recombination-rate estimates.

For each region, the focal rate was the median within 0.15 Mb. Candidate controls lay on the same arm, at least 0.50 Mb from any of the 15 focal regions. Local features were telomere-to-centromere position, log SNP density, nucleotide diversity, and H12. Feature values were converted to empirical percentiles, with position weighted 1.5-fold, and the 75 nearest controls were retained. H12 used no more than 128 deterministically spaced SNPs in each 0.30-Mb window. A 5,000-draw within-population procedure supplied descriptive permutation values. The primary summary is the median population ratio across all nine cohorts; species-specific medians are descriptive strata rather than a label-permutation test. Per-locus and leave-one-locus summaries were derived from the same frozen matched-control ratios. Figure intervals were obtained from 4,000 deterministic bootstrap resamples of populations within each species stratum; they summarize cohort heterogeneity and are not interpreted as uncertainty over independently replicated species effects.

\subsection*{Interpretation limits}

The resistance analysis compares inferred recombination-rate profiles and cannot determine causal direction. H12 matching reduces, but cannot remove, confounding by selected haplotypes, structural variation, or population history. Phase 2 does not provide the three-species contrast or the same resistance-carrier analysis available in later resources. No individual resistance region is described as independently validated by the coarse cross map.
