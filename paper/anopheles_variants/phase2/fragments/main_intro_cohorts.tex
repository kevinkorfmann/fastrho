We further investigated nine populations of the \textit{Anopheles gambiae} species complex from the open Ag1000G Phase 2 AR1 release, comprising five \textit{Anopheles gambiae} and four \textit{Anopheles coluzzii} populations \citep{malariagen_phase2_ar1_2017,ag1000g2020}. This system allowed us to study population-level variation in inferred recombination while providing maps for all five chromosome arms of each population as a community resource.
