We used \fastrho to infer population-specific recombination maps for nine Phase 2 populations across five chromosome arms. Across the 15 predefined resistance regions, resistance-associated regions had lower inferred recombination overall, although this reduction was not observed at every region or in every cohort. This observational comparison does not establish whether resistance evolution reduced recombination or whether existing recombination patterns influenced resistance evolution. Direct investigation using pedigrees, gamete sequencing, or cytology will be needed to distinguish these possibilities.
