# fastrho dog-bottleneck-v1

## Summary

`dog-bottleneck-v1` is the folded composite-LD specialist trained on village-dog and breed-like
population-size histories. It targets the long historical LD generated by severe recent canine
bottlenecks.

## Intended use

Use this checkpoint for unphased, unpolarized diploid canine genotypes when the cohort resembles the
two-mode training prior. Set `input_mode="unpolarized"`. For breeds with saturated long-range LD,
the scientifically preferred design is often to infer the conserved landscape from a colocated
village-dog or wolf panel rather than from the breed alone.

This released 15,000-region retrain uses 17 folded composite-LD features with the default
5 kb, 25 kb, and 50 kb radii. It is not the earlier prototype described with experimental megabase-
scale feature bands. The released `feat_stats.npz` records the actual `gtf` input contract.

## Training record

The retained checkpoint is epoch 17 (global step 2,808). Training mixed higher-diversity village-
dog histories with severe breed bottlenecks. Exact artifact hashes are in `manifest.json`.

## Limitations

Recent bottlenecks can erase fine-scale recombination information rather than merely bias an
estimator. Breed-only fine-scale maps can therefore be unidentifiable even with a matched model.
Validate resolution and absolute scale under a cohort-matched demographic simulation, report
whether a transfer panel was used, and do not generalize this checkpoint automatically to every
domesticated species.
